What PAPTrack Does

PAPTrack keeps track of your CPAP supplies — masks, cushions, filters, tubing, and water chambers — and tells you when it's time to clean, replace, or reorder each one. It runs entirely on your iPhone, with an optional home-screen widget and reminders.

Common Questions

Do I need an account?
No. PAPTrack works fully offline with no sign-in. If you have more than one iPhone, your data syncs automatically through your iCloud account — nothing to set up.

What's the Google sign-in in Settings for?
It's optional. Signing in with Google syncs your supply list with the companion web app at eagleadams86.github.io/paptrack, so you can view or edit your list from a browser too. If you never tap it, it's never used.

Reminders aren't showing up.
Open Settings inside the app and make sure notifications are turned on — iOS requires you to grant permission the first time. If you previously declined, the app links you straight to the iOS Settings toggle.

How do I back up or move my data?
In-app Settings has a Backup section that exports your full supply list as a JSON file saved through the Files app, and can import one back in.

How do I delete my data?
Delete items individually in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on-device. If iCloud sync was on, a copy may remain in your own iCloud account and can reappear if you reinstall. If you've also signed in with Google, email us and we'll delete your synced copy from our servers — see the Privacy Policy for details.
